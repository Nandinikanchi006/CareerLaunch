# 🚀 CareerLaunch

**CareerLaunch** is a career development platform designed to help students and fresh graduates explore career opportunities, assess their skills, discover internships and jobs, and build a strong path toward their professional goals.

## 🌟 Overview

Many students struggle with knowing:

* What career path to choose
* Which skills they need to learn
* Where to find internships
* How to prepare for jobs
* How to build a strong resume

CareerLaunch aims to bring these resources together in one simple and student-friendly platform.

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Dashboard

A personalized dashboard where users can track their career progress and explore important career resources.

### 🧭 Career Explorer

Explore different career paths and understand the skills, technologies, and opportunities associated with each career.

### 🧠 Skill Assessment

Assess your current skills and identify areas that need improvement.

### 💼 Internships

Discover internship opportunities that can help students gain practical experience.

### 🔎 Jobs

Explore job opportunities and understand the skills required for different roles.

### 📄 Resume Builder

Create and improve a professional resume based on your skills, education, projects, certifications, and experience.

### 🤖 AI Career Assistance

Future versions of CareerLaunch will include AI-powered recommendations to help users with career planning, skill recommendations, resume improvement, and interview preparation.

## 🎯 Project Goals

The main goal of CareerLaunch is to create a single platform that helps students move from:

**Learning → Skill Development → Projects → Internships → Jobs**

The platform is designed to make career planning simpler, more organized, and accessible to students.
